# Playtest 03 — developer self-test, October 16, 2026

**Build:** after the Playtests 1–2 fixes and Step 5: darkness, decoys, the noise test, the new ghost, real sound effects, title and end screens.
**Tester:** Wahab (the developer). I know the solution, so this test checks whether the level hides its clues, not whether a new player can solve it.

## What worked

- The story, dialogue and radio mechanics work as intended.
- The menus, the ending and the credits work.

## What went wrong

1. **The radio sounds were overstimulating,** especially while reading captions. The background never stops, and the radiator knocks come too often.
2. **The title screen played game audio** before I pressed Start.
3. **The crate's glow gave the radio away.** You can spot it from across the room, so there's no reason to check the other crates.
4. **The crates sat lined up alone in an empty corner.** With nothing else around them, they were the obvious place to look.
5. **The radio was found right next to the lift,** so the hot/cold test was pointless: the first thing you bang is already "warm". Hot/cold would only be rewarding if it led you somewhere you hadn't been, ideally with a red herring along the way.
6. **The rooms felt empty.** Too few objects, no layers, nothing to search through. The level needs density, not textures.
7. **Bug: the sheets overlapped.** Pulling the right sheet covered the centre sheet (the one hiding the lift), and after the right sheet moved back, the centre sheet could no longer be pulled.
8. **It still felt straightforward,** like you could rush through.

## What I changed, and why

| Problem | Change | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Overstimulating audio | Sam's radio is half as loud; radiator knocks only every 40–60 s; ambient captions show at most once per room and never over dialogue; at most two captions on screen | Quiet makes the important sounds stand out, and the player can read in peace |
| 2. Title audio | The title screen is silent apart from a faint room tone | The game starts when the player starts it |
| 3. Glow | The red light only shows from under 1 m away; the radio's feedback whine is the guide | You have to listen and search, not just spot a light |
| 4. Empty crate corner | The crates are part of a cluttered Book Fair and bake-sale corner: a folding table, boxes, a tote, a cooler, folded chairs, banners | The right crate hides among many things you could check |
| 5. Radio next to the lift | The lift moved into a new Book Room beside the library, on the wall the two rooms share. The radio is hidden 7.5 m away, on the far side of the library. Lou's reaction is set by each noise's distance to the lift, and the library shelf right behind the lift is a red herring: "Close… but not there." | Hot/cold now leads you across the library to the shelf behind the lift, then makes you work out that Lou is on the other side of the wall |
| 6. Empty rooms | A full layout from a drawn plan (`Docs/Level/DeadAir_LevelPlan.png`), with real furniture from the Kenney Furniture Kit (CC0): tall book stacks, a reading rug, carts, a card catalogue, a computer desk and decorations in the library; textbook shelving and haunted-house props in the Book Room; lockers, notices and clutter in the corridor; desks, a chalkboard and coats in Room 12 | Searching needs places to search, and a real school is full of things |
| 7. Sheet bug | The sheets moved into the Book Room: two cover piles of haunted-house props, one hangs over the lift. They sit apart and fall to the floor when pulled, so they can't overlap or be put back | Fixes the bug, and sheets over stored props make more sense than sheets hung on a library wall |
| 8. Rushing | A short pause after each of Lou's lines before Talk works again; the clutter and distances above slow the search | The player listens, and the search takes real exploring |

**Not changed:** splitting the library to make the new room. The library was already small, so the Book Room was added beside it instead.
