# Playtest 02 — developer self-test, October 16, 2026

**Build:** after Step 4, the first complete playthrough from waking up to the title card. The Playtest 1 fixes weren't in yet.
**Tester:** Wahab (the developer). I know the solution, so this test can't tell me whether the mystery is hard enough, only whether it reveals itself.

## What worked

- Everything is there and the game can be finished: the lift, the beacon and Lou's rescue all work.
- Voice dialogue should help a lot once the real recordings replace the placeholder tones.

## What went wrong

1. **You can skip the story.** Pressing Talk quickly rushes through Lou's lines, so you get to the point without listening.
2. **The mystery doesn't reveal itself.** Nothing makes you search, explore or get confused. There are no decoys yet and little to find, and with placeholder tones the radio clues (the library's sounds coming through the radio) don't register, so there's nothing to work out.
3. **Lou's ghost looked goofy,** more like a smooth cartoon robot (Baymax from Big Hero 6) than a kid under a sheet, so the scare didn't land.

## What I changed, and why

| Problem | Change | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Skippable story | Talk does nothing while a line is still playing. | Radios are half-duplex, which is already the game's rule; you can't talk over someone, so you have to listen. |
| 2. No mystery | The Playtest 1 fixes add the searching: decoy crates and sheets, a far → closer → warmer noise test, and the radio's feedback whine. On top of that: Sam's radio shows a receive light that stays on non-stop at the start (someone is stuck on talk); captions use the same words for a sound heard live and on the radio; the school is now dark, with moonlight, a lamp in the library and the back corner in shadow; and real voices and sounds are replacing the placeholder tones. | The clues in this game are sounds and lights. With beeps and full lighting, you can't notice that the radio sounds like the library or spot a glow under a crate, so the first reveal can't happen. |
| 3. Goofy ghost | A new bedsheet-ghost model: the cloth flows from the head, with folds, a ragged hem, cut-out eye holes and sneakers showing. It's matte and lit only by the lift's lamp. She's barely visible for a second, then springs up close. | A scare needs a moment of not quite seeing, and she has to read as a kid in a sheet. |

**Not changed:** I can't make the mystery feel new to myself, because I know the answer. The real test is a fresh player (Playtest 3).
