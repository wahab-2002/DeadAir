# Dead Air — Radio-play paper test (about 20 minutes)

You are the game. Your tester plays Sam. They tell you what they do; you tell them only what Sam sees and hears. You never suggest what to do.

## Before you start (5 minutes)

- On a sheet of paper, draw the map: Room 12 at the left end of a long corridor. Halfway down, the LIBRARY, with a small square in its back wall (don't label it). Beside the library, a door marked BASEMENT. At the right end, the CAFETERIA doors and the FRONT DOORS. Do **not** draw the basement rooms.
- Have this script, a pen and a timer (your phone).
- Pick something to knock on (the table) and something to rattle (keys).
- Tell the tester: "You're playing a kid. Tell me what you do and think out loud. I'll tell you what happens. There's no wrong answer."

## Rules for you

- Describe only what Sam sees or hears. If they ask "Can I…?", answer with what happens when they try.
- Read radio sounds like captions: "On your radio: hiss… bubbling… ticking."
- Don't hint before the times in the hint table. Write down every hint you give.
- Write down their exact words whenever they guess what's going on.
- If they do something not listed here, answer the way the world would, and note it. Those are often the best findings.

## Opening (start the timer, read aloud)

"You're eleven. You wake up under a pile of coats in the coat cubby of your classroom, Room 12. It's dark, just moonlight. Paper bats hang on strings, and a jack-o'-lantern with a fake candle flickers on the teacher's desk. The clock says 11:58. A walkie-talkie lies by your hand, hissing quietly. Three slow knocks come from the radiator under the window *(knock 3 times)*. A sheet of paper is taped to the cubby wall right in front of your face. You can walk around, look at things, use things, and hold your radio's button to talk."

## What to say when they do something

| If they… | You say |
| --- | --- |
| Read the paper | "THE PACT. 1. Hide till everyone leaves. 2. Meet on the CAFETERIA STAGE at 10. 3. Catch the Knocking Ghost on tape — it knocks in the walls on Halloween! 4. Radios on channel 3. Knock code: 1 = YES, 2 = NO, 3 = COME FIND ME. 5. Cover story: you're at my house, I'm at yours. 6. Whoever chickens out has to SAY IT on the radio. Signed, Lou and Sam." |
| Talk on the radio (before the crate) | "You whisper, 'Lou? I fell asleep. Are you there? Over.' No answer. Behind the hiss: bubbling… and slow ticking." |
| Touch or listen to a radiator | "It knocks three times. A pause. Three more." *(knock)* |
| Leave Room 12 | "A long, dark corridor. Every radiator knocks three times at the same moment, the whole building at once. Halfway down, warm lamplight shines through a door's windows: LIBRARY. Beside it, a heavy door. At the far end, an orange glow: the cafeteria doors, and the front entrance." |
| Go to the cafeteria | "The doors are chained shut. Through the glass: string lights, an empty stage, a sign that says HAUNTED HOUSE (CLOSED). Nobody's in there." |
| Try the basement door | "It says BASEMENT · B-1 BOILER ROOM · B-2 STORAGE · STAFF ONLY. Locked. It's warm, and there's a deep roar behind it." |
| Try the front doors | "They'd open. But Sam says, 'Not without Lou.'" |
| **About 2 minutes in, wherever they are** | "A clock chimes midnight. You hear it twice at once: live, from down the corridor toward the library, and inside your radio's hiss." |
| Enter the library | "Shelves, a reading rug, a bubbling aquarium glowing blue, a big clock. The front desk is decorated as a Haunted Library: cobwebs, a candy bowl, a plastic witch." |
| Walk toward the desk | "The witch shrieks and cackles. It's motion-activated. An instant later, the same cackle comes out of your radio. And there's a red light glowing from under a crate on the desk." |
| Lift the crate | "Under it, a yellow walkie-talkie labeled PTA #4. Its talk button was pinned down by the crate. Your radio's hiss cuts out. Silence. Then, weak and crackly: '—am? Sam, please. Anyone. Over.'" |
| Talk now (1st time) | "You say, 'Lou! It's me! Where are you?' Your voice squeals back out of the yellow radio too." Then Lou: "Sam?! I've been calling for hours. I hid to jump out at you, and then I was falling, and now it's pitch black. I don't know where I am. My flashlight broke." "Behind her voice: dripping, and a deep roar." |
| Talk again, or 15 seconds pass | Sam: "What's down there with you?" Lou: "Boxes, I think. Sheets. And something laughs every time I move." *(cackle, muffled)* "That!" |
| Talk again, or 15 seconds pass | Lou: "My batteries are almost dead. I'm gonna hold the button down so you can hear what I hear. Find me. Please find me." "Her radio stays open now: breathing, dripping, the roar. When you press talk, nothing happens; the green light stays on." |
| **While her radio is open:** pass the desk witch | "The witch cackles. A moment later, faint and muffled, the same cackle comes through Lou's radio." |
| …bang a radiator | "A faint clank in her feed. Then three knocks come back, through every radiator." *(knock 3 times)* |
| …bang the basement door | "A faint thud comes through her feed." |
| …open or touch the small door in the library's back wall, or its crank | *(rattle keys loudly)* "A loud rattle comes through her feed, right up close. Lou gasps: 'That! That's right next to me! Sam, I think that's what I fell out of… the light's blinking… please don't leave me down h—' Click. Silence." |
| …anything else | "Nothing new comes through her feed." |
| Look inside the small door | "A small wooden box on ropes: a dumbwaiter car. Inside, Lou's plastic pumpkin pail, candy spilled. A plate: SUPPLY LIFT · LIBRARY ⇄ B-2 STORAGE · OPERATE FROM THIS STATION ONLY · SET BRAKE BEFORE LOADING OR UNLOADING · CAR WILL MOVE. A note taped beside it: 'Haunted House props → B-2 tonight. B-2 gets LOCKED after. Leave the library witch out for the display! — Denise.'" |
| Try to climb in | "Sam says, 'If I go down too, there's nobody to crank us back up.'" |
| Crank the empty car down | "The rope creaks. The car sinks. Clunk at the bottom. Nothing else changes. The knocking goes on, three and three." |
| Put the yellow radio in the car and crank it down | "Creak… clunk." |
| Talk with the radio down there (1st) | "'Lou! The cupboard's back! Follow my voice!' You hear your own voice, faint, from deep in the shaft. Three knocks from below. Closer than before." *(knock softly)* "Somewhere down there, the witch cackles." |
| Talk (2nd) | "'Keep coming! You're close!' Three knocks, closer still." *(knock louder)* |
| Talk (3rd) | "'Climb in. I'll pull you up.' One knock, right on the car." *(knock once)* "The crank feels heavy now." |
| Crank up | "Slowly. The roar fades. The dripping stops. The aquarium bubbles. Clunk. You open the little door. A small bedsheet ghost is curled up inside, hugging the yellow radio. 'Boo,' she says. 'Did you get the Knocking Ghost on tape?' Sam: 'Yeah. It was you.' In the walls, the pipes go quiet." **Stop the timer.** |

## Hints (only if they're stuck, and write each one down)

| Situation | After | Sam says |
| --- | --- | --- |
| In the library, crate not lifted | 45 seconds | "Something's glowing under that crate." |
| Lou's radio is open, no noise tested | 40 seconds | "If she's holding the button, anything loud near her should come through." |
| Same, still nothing | 80 seconds | "She said she fell. Fell from where?" |
| Empty car lowered, nothing happening | 20 seconds | "She can't see it." |
| Same | 45 seconds | "She needs something to follow." |
| Same | 75 seconds | "If she could hear me down there…" |

## Write down as you go

- The time when they: leave Room 12 · enter the library · lift the crate · try their first noise · find the lift · lower the empty car · get the radio-in-the-lift idea · finish.
- Every hint you gave, and when.
- Anywhere they were stuck for more than 30 seconds, and what they were trying.
- Their exact words when they guess what's going on.

## Afterwards, ask in this order (don't explain anything until all six are answered)

1. What happened to Lou?
2. What was the "dead air" on your radio at the start?
3. How did you work out where she was?
4. Why did you put the radio in the lift? (Or: what were you trying instead?)
5. Where were you confused, or bored?
6. What did you think the knocking was at first?

## It passes if, without hints beyond the first one

- [ ] They went to the library because of what they heard on the radio.
- [ ] They connected lifting the crate to Lou suddenly coming through.
- [ ] They thought to make noise and found the lift that way.
- [ ] They got the radio-in-the-lift idea with at most one hint.
