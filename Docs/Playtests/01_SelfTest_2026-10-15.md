# Playtest 01 — developer self-test, October 15, 2026

**Build:** the gray-box after Step 3: everything up to Lou's battery dying. The lift itself (Step 4) wasn't built yet.
**Tester:** Wahab (the developer). I know the solution, so anything I call "easy" is probably easier for me than for a new player.

## What worked

- The mystery was interesting: the dead air, the stuck radio and Lou's voice breaking through.
- The idea of finding Lou by sound held my attention.

## What went wrong

1. **The stuck radio was too easy to find.** The crate sits on the first desk inside the library door, right in view, and it didn't look like it was really covering anything.
2. **The hot/cold noise test was confusing.** The radiators are in the corridor, and inside the library there was nothing to make noise with, so I couldn't tell how the feature worked.
3. **The sheet hiding the lift was too easy.** Walking near it shows "Pull the sheet aside", so there was no reason to search.
4. **After Lou's battery died and I opened the lift door, nothing told me what to do next.** The lift wasn't built yet, but it showed that this moment needs a clear goal.
5. **The sounds were too loud and not eerie.** The sound when I bang a radiator didn't match Lou's knocks, even though it's meant to be the same pipe.
6. **The clocks stayed stuck at 11:58.**
7. **The school felt small.** I always knew where to go.
8. **The radio I carried went through walls and desks.**
9. **I didn't see why I'd pick up the second radio**, since Sam has one and Lou has one.

## What I changed, and why

| Problem | Change | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Radio too easy | The crate is now a slatted crate turned upside down over the radio, which lies on its side with the crate's edge pinning its talk button. It's in the library's back corner behind a shelf, next to two identical crates of pumpkin bread. The red glow is dimmer and only leaks through the slats up close. Sam's radio whines with feedback when you get near the stuck one. | Finding the radio becomes a short search, and the radio itself is the guide, which is the game's rule in action. The crate now explains why the radio was stuck. |
| 2. Hot/cold unclear | Lou's third line now explains the game ("Bang on stuff, Sam. I'll tell you if it's close"). Added things to bang on in the library (a desk bell, shelves) and the corridor (lockers, locked doors), each a step on a far → closer → warmer scale. | The rule is clear; where she is still has to be discovered. |
| 3. Sheet too easy | Three identical sheets, two of them decoys, and no prompt just for walking near. Decoys get "Close... but not there." | The hot/cold game, not proximity, decides which sheet to pull. |
| 4. No next step | Built into the lift (Step 4): Sam says the goal when the lift door is open after the battery dies, the crank is large and lit, and the first rescue hint comes after 15 s. | The player always has a clear next action. |
| 5. Sound | Overall mix turned down by about a third. All knocking uses one metallic pipe sound. | Quieter is eerier, and one sound teaches "we're on the same pipe". |
| 6. Clocks | Both clocks run from 11:58, and the midnight chime rings when they reach 12:00. | The midnight chime makes sense. |
| 7. Small school | Added three locked classroom doors along the corridor (Rooms 11, 14 and 15). They also work as far-away noises for the hot/cold game. | The school feels bigger without new rooms. |
| 8. Radio clipping | The held radio sits closer to the camera, inside the player's collision, with its collider off while held. | It can't poke into walls or desks any more. |
| 9. Why take radio #4 | Sam says what it is when the crate comes off ("Mom's PTA radio. It was stuck on talk the whole time."). Nothing pushes you to pick it up until the lift. | Its job only appears at the lift: with Lou's battery dead, radio #4 is the only way to put Sam's voice at the bottom of the shaft, where she can follow it. |

**Not changed:** a longer corridor and extra rooms. With one day left, locked doors give most of the feeling for a fraction of the work, and new rooms would mean new lighting, collisions and testing.
