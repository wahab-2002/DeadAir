## Interpretation

A partial crank journey retains its direction until an endpoint. At an endpoint, release E before starting the opposite journey. The same direction rule applies if the player looks away and comes back. Beacon progress survives early returns. Spoken lines queue so the mandatory goal cannot be lost behind another line. Recorded clips can be assigned later without changing code.

## Manual Editor work

No scene wiring is required. Optional recordings go in `Assets/_DeadAir/Audio/Voice/<id>.wav`. Run **Dead Air → Assign Voice Clips**, then save the scene. Generated placeholders are deliberately unintelligible; read the captions.

## How to test in Play mode

1. Open `Assets/_DeadAir/Scenes/DeadAir.unity`, enter Play and click the Game view. Use WASD, mouse look, E, and Q/right mouse for Talk.
2. For a normal run, call from Room 12; walk through the corridor into the library; lift the real crate and take #4. Talk through Lou's exchanges, releasing Talk for her replies. During her open feed, pull the lift's sheet or touch its door/crank/car. Wait for the click and Sam's call; open the lift for the mandatory goal.
3. Look at the pail and try the empty car. Hold E on the red wheel, release partway, then resume. Leave the empty car down for at least 80 seconds to hear/read all three hints.
4. Raise the car, open it and put #4 inside. Lower it. First wait without Talk; then press/release Talk three separate times, allowing each response to finish. Raise it and open the door. Confirm the lunge, dialogue, fade, sentence and held title.
5. Test an early return after the first beacon response, then lower again. The next Talk should produce the second response.
6. Repeat with the Editor's **Mute Audio** enabled. Dialogue, lift motion, knocks, witch, ambient transition and sting must remain understandable from captions.
7. Editor shortcuts: **F6** restarts into battery dead at the lift, with #4 available on the desk. **F7** restarts with #4 already at the bottom, ready for Talk. These keys are excluded from player builds. On keyboards using media keys, use Fn with the function key if needed.
