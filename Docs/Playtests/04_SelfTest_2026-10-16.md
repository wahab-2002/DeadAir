# Playtest 04 — developer self-test, October 16, 2026

**Build:** after the Playtest 3 level pass: the Book Room with the lift, the cluttered library, Kenney furniture, the distance-based noise test and the quieter radio.
**Tester:** Wahab (the developer).

## What worked

- The game plays much better: the rooms feel like a real school, and the search and the noise test both work.
- Holding E to turn the crank feels fine.
- I moved some furniture by hand where the placement looked odd. The layout is passable now.

## What went wrong

1. **The title menu's background is see-through.** The pact behind it shows through, and its text blends with the menu text.
2. **Lou's ghost pokes through the lift door** before it opens.
3. **The hot/cold steps are too coarse.** Almost the whole library says "Warmer.", so you can't tell how close you are. It should get warmer only as you approach the right spot.
4. **You can bang things from far away.** Standing in one place, you can aim at several objects across the room and bang them all.
5. **Nothing tells a new player to use the radio.** They might just start walking around.
6. **The background sounds are still harsh and in your face,** especially the radio and the witch's cackle.
7. **The crates are still easy to find.** They sit together in one corner near the library door, and the radio's whine starts as soon as you walk in.
8. **The Book Room is the first door you see after leaving Room 12.** A player can walk in, pull the sheets and find the lift before the mystery has even started.
9. **The opening has no story.** The pact explains the plan, but nothing says who you are or why you're asleep in a school.
10. **The school could use one more space to explore,** with a red herring of its own.

## What I changed, and why

| Problem | Change | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. See-through menu | The title menu gets a near-opaque dark panel | The menu reads as one thing, not on top of the pact |
| 2. Ghost clipping | Lou is hidden until the lift door is open, and her curled pose fits inside the car | The jump-out stays a surprise |
| 3. Coarse hot/cold | Narrower distance bands. Lou now compares each noise with the last one: closer gets "Closer… I think." or "Warmer.", farther gets "That was far away." The knock in her radio also grows from faint to loud with distance, and its caption says so | Every bang tells you whether you're getting closer, which is what hot/cold is |
| 4. Banging from afar | You have to be within about 1.2 m of something to bang it | Finding her means walking around, not standing still |
| 5. No radio hint | A small hint at the top left: "Hold Q or right mouse to talk on your radio", until you first talk | The first action is clear without a tutorial |
| 6. Harsh sound | Sam's radio quieter again, the witch cackle quieter and shorter-range, the knocks a little softer, and a softer radio filter | Quiet makes the important sounds stand out |
| 7. Crates easy to find | Three crates spread around the library, away from the door; the whine only starts within 2.5 m | Finding the radio becomes a real search |
| 8. Book Room found first | The Book Room's corridor door is removed. Its only door is now a staff door behind the library's circulation desk | You reach the lift through the library, where the clues lead, not by accident |
| 9. No story | After Start, a short text card sets the scene before you wake up | The player knows who they are and why they're there |
| 10. More to explore | If time allowed: Room 14 opens as a second classroom with a red herring, a bedsheet-ghost costume slumped on a chair | A false "is that Lou?" moment that fits the Halloween setting |
